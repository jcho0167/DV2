How do artists earn their income?

Live performances dominate the income breakdown for artists, followed by royalties. Income earned from live performances isn't only reserved for the top artists.


Additionally, analysis from Music Australia across 2023-24 and 2024-25 shows that artists earning lower incomes tended to be more reliant on live performances for their income. Higher income artists tended to earn a higher proportion of their incomes from royalties.

Where do Australian Music Artists Mostly Play At ?

Hotels and pubs account for the largest number of performances in the supplied data. Comparing venue types helps show how live music is distributed across everyday venues, dedicated performance spaces and private settings.


Stadiums and arenas are predominantly reserved for higher income top artists, while lower income artists rely on performing in pubs, hotels, and theatres to make a regular income. This reflects how skewed the income distribution for artists in Australia are, there is a much larger proportion of lower income artists.

How Do Artists Access Labels and Distribution ?

The largest group reports having no label or distribution support. Other artists report access to selected distribution services, their own label, or an independent or major label. This gives context to the different routes artists may take to release music.

Which Music Subindustries Earn Revenue Overseas ?

The bubble chart compares 2025 total revenue with export revenue for the many music subindustries. Larger bubbles represent higher total revenue, while colour shows export revenue as a percentage of total revenue. This helps distinguish the biggest sectors from those with a greater export orientation.


As seen in the previous tree map, live performances had the greatest total revenue, however export revenue is quite limited. This is intuitive, as live performances are the main income source for the disproportionately larger number of low income artists.
