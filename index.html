Where the music comes from

The ARIA Music Awards have been Australia's main music prize since 1987. We took the 53 artists who have won six or more of them and traced each one back to the town where they grew up or formed.

Three of Australia's most-awarded acts come from Yolŋu communities in Arnhem Land.

Side A · Track 02

The trophy cabinet

Being nominated is an honour; winning is another matter. Some artists collect awards almost every time they are nominated, while others rack up dozens of nominations.

John Farnham: 22 wins from 65 nominations, the most of anyone on the list.

Side B · Track 03

The home crowd

Every year, Triple J listeners vote for their favourite songs of the past year in the Hottest 100. It is often called the world's biggest music poll, and it shows how much Australians listen to their own artists.

When the yearly countdown began in 1993, only 24 of the 100 songs were Australian. Local music climbed steadily, peaking at 66 songs in 2016 and again in 2020. In 2024 it fell to 29 as US and UK pop stars dominated the vote, then bounced back to 54 in 2025.

In 2024 only 29 of the 100 songs were Australian, the fewest since 1996.

Side B · Track 04

Going global

A handful of Australian songs have become worldwide hits. We followed four of the biggest, released between 2001 and 2021, through the national charts of every country listed on their Wikipedia pages.

Tones and I's Dance Monkey is the standout, reaching No. 1 from Germany to Malaysia to Peru. Kylie Minogue's Can't Get You Out of My Head topped almost every chart it entered in 2001, although fewer countries had official charts back then.

Dance Monkey reached No. 1 in 37 of the 60 countries where it charted.

Encore

What we learned

Talent is everywhere

Melbourne and Sydney produce the most stars, but per person the Northern Territory and South Australia lead the country.

Home support swings

Australian songs rose from a quarter to two-thirds of the Hottest 100, then dropped sharply in 2024 before recovering.

The world is listening

Four songs alone reached No. 1 in 45 countries, mostly in Europe and the Americas.

Notes on method

"Home town" uses the Origin field of each artist's Wikipedia infobox. Where that is missing, it uses where the artist grew up, and failing that, their Australian birthplace. Chart positions use each country's main national chart and ignore genre and airplay sub-charts unless a country has no other chart listed. All figures were collected on 19 October 2026 and may change as the sources are updated.
